Where to find your specs

Windows: the quick way
  1. CPU, memory, drives, graphics: press Ctrl+Shift+Esc to open Task Manager, then open the Performance tab. Click CPU, Memory, Disk and GPU to see each model and size.
  2. Motherboard: press Win+R, type msinfo32, press Enter. Look for BaseBoard Manufacturer and BaseBoard Product. A prebuilt may show the PC maker's name instead.
  3. RAM type and speed: Task Manager → Performance → Memory shows speed and type (DDR4 or DDR5) in the top right.
Windows: the detailed way

The free tool CPU-Z has tabs for CPU, Mainboard, Memory and Graphics. It lists exact models and speeds. Copy what you see into the boxes below.

Mac or Linux

On a Mac, choose the Apple menu → About This Mac. On Linux, lscpu, free -h, lsblk and lspci | grep -i vga show CPU, memory, drives and graphics.
